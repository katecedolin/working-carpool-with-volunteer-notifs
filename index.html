How it works

  1. Paste your Google Sheets link to the Responses tab created by your form.
  2. Enter your total event capacity. This limits the number of attendees we assign (drivers, self-drivers, riders combined).
  3. Click Build Carpool, then use Schedule Reminders to text everyone.

We expect these column headers (case-insensitive): Name, Transportation?, and If you can provide transportation for others. A column containing “Phone” is used for numbers.
